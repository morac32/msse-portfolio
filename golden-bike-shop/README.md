## The System

Golden Bike Shop is a regional business with three connected operations:

- **Retail** – online and in-store sales of bikes, parts, and accessories across four locations with shared inventory
- **Rental** – a tracked fleet of about 400 bikes rented hourly, daily, or weekly, including e-bikes with telemetry for location and battery state
- **Service** – a shared workshop booking system covering both customer bikes and the rental fleet

This package is the integrated software design for a platform that connects all three operations.
